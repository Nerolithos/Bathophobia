## Important distinctions / 关键区分

- α-quartz: trigonal within the hexagonal crystal family; six-sided habit is not a separate crystal system. Agate is an aggregate, amethyst/citrine are quartz varieties, and morganite is beryl.
- Autunite: older Handbook tables use tetragonal symmetry. Locock & Burns (2003), *American Mineralogist* 88, 240–244, resolved synthetic fully hydrated material as orthorhombic Pnma (11 waters), pseudotetragonal. The catalog uses this structural result and the conventional 10–12 hydration range; dehydration changes the phase.
- Iris agate: transmitted-light diffraction, not luminescence. Animated glow is artistic gameplay presentation.
- Anapaite is not “shell phosphate”; association with fossil material does not imply every submerged shell forms it.
- Obsidian and coal are rocks without a unique formula/crystal system. Processed glass, crude metals and building materials are not mineral species.
- Crafting reactions, radiation dizziness, spawn locations and item glows are game abstractions, not laboratory procedures or universal geological claims.
